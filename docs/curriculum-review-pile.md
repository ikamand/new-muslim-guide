# Curriculum update — the review pile

**Written 8 Sep 2026**, the day the last page landed. Every page below is
model-written English over citations that were opened in the corpus before
they were printed. The citations are checked. The **substance** is not, and
this is the list of what a qualified reader has to clear, ranked by what
goes wrong if it is wrong. Iyad reads it in one sitting.

Two things apply to every page:

- **The Muslim numbers.** Six new Muslim citations (1006, 1349, 1437, 1631,
  2664, 2996) were resolved by the evidence script through the collection's
  own numbering, and the rendered translation of each was read to confirm
  it is the right narration. Worth a glance at the text on screen anyway.
- **Every `differs` note's positions were written from the author's
  understanding**, not read from a fiqh text. There are three, all named
  below. They are the first things to check.

---

## 1. I made a mistake and finished praying — `learn/realised-after.ts`

Book 1 · When it goes wrong · last. Every branch is a ruling.

- **A doubt after finishing is disregarded.** Stated as "the scholars'
  rule", from the principle that certainty is not removed by doubt, with
  Muslim 571a (build on what you are sure of) as the narration for doubt
  during prayer, extended to after. Is the extension stated correctly?
- **The differs note**: whether a prayer that needed sujud al-sahw and did
  not get them must be repeated. Printed: Hanafi, required and the prayer
  is repeated; Shafiʿi, recommended and the prayer stands. ⚠️ Unchecked.
  Maliki and Hanbali are not printed at all.
- **A pillar missed**: "if you realise while still in your place and only a
  moment has passed, stand and complete what was missed, then make the two
  prostrations; if it has been a while, pray it again." Cited to Bukhari
  757. Is "complete it" right for every pillar, and is "a while" left to a
  person the right call?
- **Prayed without wudu**: repeat, whenever realised, Bukhari 135. Stated
  as true "whether you knew at the time or worked it out afterwards".
- **Impurity found on clothes afterwards**: named and sent to a person,
  with no ruling. Is naming it without answering it acceptable?
- Bukhari 1229 (Dhul-Yadain) is used for "prostrated twice after the salam
  when he ended a prayer early". Check the paraphrase.

## 2. Intimacy, modesty and purification — `learn/intimacy.ts`

Book 2 · People · after "If you already have a partner". Sections 4 and 5
are rulings.

- **"There is no third category"** beside marriage, from Qur'an 23:5–6.
  The verse's "those their right hands possess" is not mentioned. Is the
  simplification acceptable for a beginner's page?
- **Two things ruled out**: intercourse during menstruation (2:222) and
  anal intercourse (Abu Dawud 2162, hasan by al-Albani, Abdul Hamid,
  Zubair Ali Zai). Both stated as held across the schools in an `agreed`
  note.
- **"Beyond those, and the daylight hours of a fast, the religion does not
  hand married people a rulebook."** Is that too broad? Iḥrām is not
  mentioned.
- **Ghusl is due "whether or not anything was released"**: Bukhari 291.
  **Wudu before sleeping while junub**: Bukhari 288, stated as what the
  Prophet ﷺ did, not as required.
- **"Wudu on its own does not lift the state."** Stated flatly.
- The duʿa before intimacy (Bukhari 141) is linked to Hisn occasion
  1269543 rather than printed. Confirm the occasion is the right one.
- Muslim 1437 paraphrased as "the man who goes to his wife, and she to him,
  and then he tells others what passed between them". Check.

## 3. What happens after death — `learn/after-death.ts`

Book 3 · What you believe more deeply · fourth. Creed, and the register
was the instruction: not fear-based.

- **The grave**: only Bukhari 1338 is used, and only what it says: two
  angels, the question about "this man", the believer's answer, shown a
  place in the Fire and given Paradise instead. The three-question form
  (Lord, religion, prophet) is not printed. Is leaving it out acceptable,
  or does a reviewer want it named?
- **"The answer is the second half of the tashahhud."** The narration's
  answer is "I testify that he is Allah's slave and His Messenger"; the
  tashahhud says "and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His
  messenger". Is calling them the same fair?
- **The practical note**: "People will tell you frightening things about
  the grave, some of them from narrations and some not. What is above is
  what the Prophet ﷺ said the believer meets." Is that a fair summary, or
  does it understate the punishment of the grave in a way a reviewer would
  not sign?
- **Muslim 1631** (three things continue): "a righteous child who prays for
  them" is printed without saying the deceased is Muslim. For a convert
  with non-Muslim parents this may read as a promise about them.
- **Bukhari 6463**: "nobody's deeds alone will save them, not even his,
  unless Allah bestows His mercy." Check the paraphrase against the text.
- **Bukhari 7404**: "what Allah wrote about Himself when He created
  everything: that His mercy overcomes His anger." Check.
- The Fire is deliberately not described. Is one sentence enough?

## 4. Qadr — `learn/qadr.ts`

Book 3 · What you believe more deeply · fifth.

- **The hero states four things**: "He knew it, it was written, He willed
  that it come to be, and He created it." The four levels without their
  names, cited to 57:22 and 54:49. Is that a fair statement of the
  article, and are those the right verses for it?
- **"You cannot sin and blame the register"**, from 6:148. Check that the
  verse is being used for what it says.
- **Muslim 2664** paraphrased: strive, seek help, do not lose heart, do not
  say "if only", say "Allah decreed it and what He willed He did". Check
  the last clause against the text.
- **Tirmidhi 2516** (sahih, al-Albani) and **2517** (hasan, al-Albani):
  the grades are the corpus's and were read. Confirm they are acceptable
  for a page that prints them.
- **"This is the article the scholars wrote the most about."** Repeated
  from the six-articles record. Is it defensible as written?
- The historical argument is not entered and the four levels are not
  named. Is that the right depth for Book 3?

## 5. Hajj and Umrah — `learn/hajj-and-umrah.ts`

Book 2 · The year · after Eid.

- ⚠️ **Corrected the day it was written**: the first draft said Umrah is
  "never required". Now a `differs` note: Hanafi and Maliki, recommended;
  Shafiʿi and Hanbali, required once in a lifetime for whoever is able.
  **From the author's understanding, unchecked.** Second thing to check
  on the whole list.
- **What "able" means**: physically able to travel, money for the journey
  without leaving debts or dependants unprovided for, and a safe road.
  Stated as "the scholars' reading" with a general note that details
  differ. Is the summary right?
- Whether Hajj must be made the first year one is able is deliberately
  not stated. Whether a woman travels with a maḥram is deliberately not
  stated. Is silence on both acceptable?
- **Bukhari 1521** paraphrased "without intimacy and without sin during
  it, comes back as he was on the day his mother bore him". Check.
- "Stand together on the plain of ʿArafah through one afternoon" is
  uncited orientation.

## 6. The revealed books — `learn/the-revealed-books.ts`

Book 3 · What you believe more deeply · third.

- **The taḥrīf position**, in "Is that the Bible I grew up with?": the
  books were sent; what circulates today is not guaranteed to be them as
  sent; cited to 2:79, with an `agreed` note calling this the settled
  Sunni position. Is the wording right, and gentle enough without being
  wrong?
- **"Nothing of Ibrahim's scrolls survives."** Uncited.
- **"The Arabic you recite in prayer is the same Arabic recited fourteen
  centuries ago"**, from 15:9. Fair?
- **"Five, given to five prophets"**: the scrolls of Ibrahim, Tawrat,
  Zabur, Injil, Qur'an. 87:19 names the scriptures of Ibrahim *and Musa*;
  the page lists Ibrahim's scrolls and the Tawrat separately. Is the count
  and the pairing acceptable?
- The advice section on how to answer a relative quoting the Bible: check
  the register.

## 7. The prophets — `learn/the-prophets.ts`

Book 3 · What you believe more deeply · second.

- **"Adam: the first man and the first prophet."** The Qur'an does not call
  Adam a prophet in those words; the narration that does is not cited.
- **"Musa: named in the Qur'an more than any other."** A count, flagged in
  a general note as "the standard one". Confirm.
- **"The laws given to each differed in detail. The thing being taught did
  not."** From 21:25 and 42:13. Fair?
- The difference between nabī and rasūl is not taught; the count of
  twenty-five names is not printed. Acceptable?
- 4:125 is used for "Allah took Ibrahim as an intimate friend" and 2:127
  for the Kaʿbah. Check the bullet's "the line of prophets after him all
  run back to him".

## 8. The angels — `learn/angels.ts`

Book 3 · What you believe more deeply · first.

- **"Made from light"**: Muslim 2996. **"Never disobey"**: 66:6, whose
  context is the angels over the Fire. Is using it for angels generally
  acceptable?
- **"The two recorders: one on each side."** Cited to 82:10–12, which says
  keepers, noble, recording. "One on each side" is 50:17, not cited.
- **Bukhari 445** paraphrased as "while you sit in the place you prayed,
  before you move, they ask forgiveness for you". The narration says as
  long as he is at his place of prayer and does not break wudu. The
  paraphrase drops the second condition.
- **Bukhari 555** paraphrased: shifts meet at Fajr and ʿAsr; those who
  stayed the night ascend and are asked; they answer "we left them praying
  and found them praying". Check.
- "Not the souls of the dead" is uncited orientation.

## When extra prayers are held off — `learn/held-off-times.ts`

Added 10 Sep 2026, after the prayer-times audit. Book 1 · When it goes
wrong · after "Missing a prayer". Every section is a ruling; the full item
is `docs/scholarly-review.md` §1.12.

- **The three times** from Sahih Muslim 831, with "about a quarter of an
  hour" for the first. Is the gloss right?
- **"The five, and a missed one, are never held off"**: Bukhari 597,
  extended to the sun-bound times by the majority position. The differs
  note prints Hanafi against Shafiʿi, with Hanbali and the contemporary
  view folded into the second. ⚠️ Positions written from the fatwas cited,
  not from a fiqh text.
- **The after-Fajr and after-ʿAsr spans**: Bukhari 586. Stated as the
  reason the Every prayer page shows no sunnah after those two.
- **The reason**: Bukhari 3273, "between the two horns of a devil". Check
  the paraphrase, and whether the page should carry it at all.
